# Audits

A monthly audit keeps the patterns and checks useful:

1. Review official source URLs and guidance changes.
2. Run a full scan against representative projects.
3. Review new, repeated, unresolved, and false-positive findings.
4. Compare results with native tools.
5. Sample production and test files, including AI-generated changes.
6. Revise, downgrade, deprecate, or remove noisy checks.
7. Record patterns changed, sources reviewed, owners, decisions, and next review date.

Use the [monthly audit template](https://github.com/sayanmohsin/nice-code/blob/main/docs/monthly-audit.md). Keep detailed reports in local or CI artifacts.
